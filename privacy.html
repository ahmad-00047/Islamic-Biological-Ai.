What we process

When you ask the AI tutor a question, the text you submit is sent to the configured AI service so it can generate an answer. Do not submit passwords, identity documents, private images or sensitive personal information.

Voice

If you use voice input, your browser or device speech service may process the audio to produce text. Voice output is generated on your device through available speech features.

Results

The BISE Bahawalpur result button opens the official board website. The app does not claim to access, alter or bypass board security and does not permanently store roll numbers in this prototype.

Storage and sharing

We do not sell personal information. AI providers may process submitted questions to provide the requested response according to their own policies. Avoid entering personal information in questions.

Children and safety

This is an educational app. Biology and reproductive-health topics are presented for learning in age-appropriate, modest and safety-conscious language. Contact a qualified teacher, doctor or guardian for personal advice.
